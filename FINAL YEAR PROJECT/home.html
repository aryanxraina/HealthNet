Your Data Privacy & Security

We prioritize the security and privacy of your health data with privacy-first design and strong security practices.

End-to-End Encryption

Your health data is encrypted both in transit and at rest, ensuring it remains secure at all times.

User Consent Control

You have complete control over who can access your health data and for what purpose.

Privacy by Design

Built to collect only the data needed for your insights, with privacy considered at every step.

Biometric Authentication

Secure access to your health data using fingerprint or facial recognition.

Our Data Privacy Commitment

Your data is yours. We never sell or share your personal health information with third parties without your explicit consent.

We use anonymized data for AI model training to improve health predictions, but you can opt out at any time.

Our security measures are regularly audited by independent third-party experts to ensure the highest standards.

You can request a complete export or deletion of your data at any time through your account settings.
